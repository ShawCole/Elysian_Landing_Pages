How this gets deployed

In StackAdapt, build four distinct audience segments — one per avatar. Paste each avatar's intent keywords into the keyword-targeting layer of the segment, and the recoil keywords into the brand-safety / negative-targeting layer. The native phrases are not for targeting — they're for ad copy and the LP hero, where they earn the click after the targeting puts the ad in front of the right person.

Audience-size health check: 5,000+ qualified intent-positive users per avatar = green. Below 2,000 = too narrow (broaden the keyword set). Above 50,000 = too broad (the targeting won't pull weight against efficient cohorts). StackAdapt reports estimated reach during segment build — adjust until each avatar lands in the green band before activating.

Optimization loop: Weekly review of CTR per avatar. If an avatar's CTR drops below 0.5%, iterate the keyword set first (likely too broad or off-axis), iterate creative second. If recoil-list audiences are accidentally getting served, add the offending phrases to brand-safety.
