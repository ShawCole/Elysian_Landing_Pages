No one has done this exact thing publicly

This is uncharted territory. We're going to learn by doing.

We searched for case studies of high-ticket Enagic / Amway / network-marketing-adjacent businesses scaled via DSP and programmatic. They effectively don't exist — for three honest reasons: top operators in this niche don't publish playbooks (compliance + competitive), DSPs don't curate MLM-adjacent customers into their public lineup, and the established channels for this category have historically been organic social and team-builds, not programmatic.

We have plausible reasons to believe DSP transfers from adjacent categories (B2B lead gen, financial services, high-ticket coaching) — but no public confirmation. So this rollout is structured as a series of small, instrumented experiments rather than a deployment of a known-winner playbook. Every channel has explicit go/no-go thresholds. We scale what works, kill what doesn't, redirect spend weekly. The architecture is sound; the channel mix is the experiment.

What we're not pretending

Honest assessment of the risks

The three real risks

  • Meta shadowban probability: 30–40% in the first 30 days of paid biz-opp DM ads if run from a personal-linked Page. Mitigation: dedicated Brand Page, organic-first, paid Meta deferred until the page has 60+ days of clean engagement.
  • Enagic distributor warning: 15–25% probability by month 4–6 if scaling paid traffic visibly. The DSSRC just hit Enagic for distributor income claims. Mitigation: zero specific dollar amounts in cold copy; earnings disclaimer footed; no Kangen/Enagic mentions in public ads ever.
  • Channel concentration: Original plan over-relied on Meta. Revised plan: organic + DSP + email/podcast sponsorships are the durable scale levers. Meta is an accelerator if it works, not a foundation.

If we're being honest about pace

  • The 60-min masterclass drops watch-through ~40% vs a 30-min cut. Strong recommendation to edit down before launch — the longer version becomes the post-call asset for closers, not the top-of-funnel commitment.
  • Phase 1 paid DMs may produce expensive noise. Realistic Phase 1 CAC: $4–7k/sale. Tightens to $2.5–4.5k by Phase 3 as organic compounds.
  • Organic compounds slowly. Plan target 4–9 closes in 90 days is honest. Steady-state of 4–7 closes/month by month 4 is realistic, not 6–10.
  • The single biggest variable is Shane's risk tolerance on Enagic's third-party advertising policy. That answer changes the channel mix.

Compliance Commitments

Protecting your accounts and your brand

The DSSRC ruling against Enagic (Case #216-2025) is fresh — every piece of copy goes through a do / don't filter before it ships.

No specific dollar amounts in cold ads. No health claims, ever. No "Kangen" or "Enagic" in public copy. Earnings disclaimer prominently footed on every page that mentions commissions. Direct Meta-cold-ad → landing page is permanently off-limits — only DSP and DM-link traffic touches the LP.
