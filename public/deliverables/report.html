01

The Business

Elysian Leaders is Shane & Vanessa Carling's team within Enagic, a 50-year-old Japanese water ionizer company. The core physical product is the Kangen Water K8 machine (medical-grade water ionizer, 5 pH levels), the Anespa shower attachment, and Ukon turmeric supplements. Machines retail at $4,000–$6,000.

How the Money Works

Enagic pays 54% of every product sale in direct commissions, divided across 8 "points" (~$350 each). Starting with the "Trifecta" package earns 2 points per sale ($1,320). The "Quad" earns 3 points ($2,565+). The comp plan pays 8 levels deep with permanent ranks — no deranking, no monthly quotas.

The Residual Income Tier

At rank 6A2-3 and above, distributors unlock $5,000–$80,000/month in passive residual income, plus milestone bonuses from $3,000 to $50,000.

Marketing Positioning

All external marketing leads with the business opportunity and lifestyle — positioned as a "high-ticket distribution engine," a "Freedom Blueprint," and a "Professional Pivot." Product specifics (Enagic, Kangen, water ionizer) are introduced in the masterclass after the prospect has been qualified through conversation.

Channel Compliance & Messaging Guide

We structured the messaging across four channels, each with its own compliance rules. The messaging becomes progressively more detailed as prospects move deeper into the funnel and demonstrate commitment.

Tier 3 — Most Restricted
Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram)

Public ads on Facebook/Instagram. Meta's platform policies require careful messaging around business opportunities.

Can Say
  • Lifestyle aspiration
  • Curiosity hooks
  • Identity challenges
  • Value-based content leading to conversation
Cannot Say
  • Income claims
  • Health claims
  • Biz-op language
  • Dollar amounts
  • "Passive income"
  • MLM signals, before/after framing
DSP — Outside Meta Ecosystem
Demand Side Platform (Programmatic Display)

IAB display ads served across open web inventory (NYT, Pinterest, etc.) via programmatic DSP. Entirely outside Meta's ad platform — no Andromeda algorithm, no Meta policy enforcement. Traffic lands directly on landing pages.

Can Say
  • Direct value propositions
  • High-ticket business language
  • "Build income that fits your life"
  • Avatar-specific hook copy
  • Lifestyle imagery + aspirational CTAs
Still Avoid
  • Specific income claims (FTC)
  • Enagic/Kangen by name (funnel strategy)
  • Health claims (FTC/FDA)
  • Guarantees or "get rich" language
Tier 2 — Moderate ("Andromeda-Unbound")
VSL / Landing Pages

Reached via DSP ads or Click-to-DM conversations. These pages are not directly linked from Meta ad units, allowing more direct messaging about the opportunity.

Can Say
  • Direct "you" address
  • Emotional agitation
  • Visceral pain language
  • Named frameworks ("The 100K Roadmap," "The Freedom Blueprint")
  • High-ticket language without specific amounts
Cannot Say
  • Specific income numbers
  • Enagic/Kangen by name
  • Health claims
  • "Passive income" (use "residual" or "leveraged")
Tier 1 — Most Permissive
The Masterclass (Private, Invite-Only)

Private, invite-only, shared after qualification conversation. No advertising platform governs this content; audience is pre-qualified.

Can Say
  • Everything — specific dollars
  • Enagic by name
  • Comp plan details
  • Health claims
  • Income testimonials with numbers
No Restrictions
  • No platform governance at this tier

The Modular VSL Architecture

The VSL is a 5-minute video with a modular "splice" architecture. Production efficiency: 4 hooks + 4 handshakes + 1 body = 9 clips serving 4 complete VSLs. Hooks can be swapped and tested without re-filming.

A
The Hook (0:00–1:15) — 4 Versions

Each avatar gets its own hook using the "Mirror Technique" — reflecting the avatar's exact internal monologue in the first 10 seconds. 6–7 versions were tested per avatar before narrowing to 2.

B
The Handshake Transition (~10 seconds)

Buffer clip where the solo presenter introduces their partner to create a "United Front." Smooths the transition from single-speaker emotional hook to dual-presenter logical body.

C
The Evergreen Master Body (1:15–5:00) — 1 Version

Single standardized section all avatar paths share. Follows the S.I.G.N.A.L. Framework:

The S.I.G.N.A.L. Framework

SStory — The Logic Gap / Time-for-Money Trap
IInfrastructure — 4 Pillars: High-Yield Assets, Global Help, Inbound Flow, System Growth
GGrowth — Shane + Vanessa's personal story
NNarrowing — Selective Audit / Qualification / Takeaway
AAction — CTA to GHL Alignment Audit
LLegacy — "The next year of your life depends on the choice you make today"

Key Strategic Principles

"Smart Peer" Tone

Shane and Vanessa must sound like equals who found a better vehicle — never gurus, never coaches, never recruiters. The target audience ($75K+ professionals) instantly rejects anyone positioning above them.

4-Syllable Word Limit

No word in any VSL script exceeds 4 syllables. Prevents scripts from sounding "written." Forces natural conversational language.

The "Mirror Technique"

First 10 seconds must reflect the avatar's EXACT internal monologue. "You've built something that works... but you ARE the business" is a mirror. "Are you tired of working hard?" is NOT.

Commitment Modal Theory

The "Yes, I Commit" click before VSL playback is deliberate friction. Fewer viewers, but psychologically committed ones who convert at much higher rates.

Friction Filters Quality

The entire funnel uses escalating commitment gates. By the time someone reaches a call, they have passed 6+ filters. The funnel does most of the selling before a human speaks.

"Andromeda-Unbound" Loophole

The foundational architectural decision. Meta's algorithm crushes direct biz-op ads. By routing through conversations or DSP channels, landing pages exist where Meta cannot see or penalize. Without this, all external copy would need to be as neutered as the ads.

"Not Broke But Broke"

Target is NOT financially desperate people. It is professionals earning $75K–$120K who feel professionally capped. They have money but no time. Ideal prospects have 650+ credit scores and disposable attention.

Narrative Transportation

The VSL does not try to "convince." It transports the viewer into a story where the conclusion feels self-discovered. The Logic Gap section creates an "aha moment" that feels organic, not sold.

The Takeaway / Selective Audit

The VSL deliberately tells viewers "this isn't for you if..." — reversing the power dynamic. Instead of the viewer being skeptical, the offer screens the viewer. Triggers desire through scarcity and status.
